ABOUT ME

THE MINER'S LOGBOOK

I'm Iain McLellan — a platform engineer who spends his working life underground, in the caverns nobody volunteers for: Azure DevOps pipelines, Terraform state, hybrid onboarding, and the 3 AM jobs everyone forgot they own.

The pattern never changes. Somebody is doing something by hand, at scale, at an unreasonable hour. I wire it to an API, put it behind a reusable workflow with federated identity, give it logging, drift detection and a rollback — and hand the hours back. A twelve-hour release now finishes in under one. Compliance evidence collects itself. Nobody gets paged for a tag change.

Favourite tools: Terraform, Bicep, Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, Azure Monitor and Azure Arc. Favourite architecture: federated identity over secrets, policy-as-code over tribal knowledge, private endpoints by default, and docs a stranger can follow at 2 AM.
