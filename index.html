I'm Matt Balzan — a Microsoft cloud and endpoint engineer who spends his days turning manual, repetitive IT work into automation that runs itself. Intune, Entra ID, Microsoft Graph, Azure Data Factory, PowerShell and a growing pile of Copilot agents.

Most of what I build starts the same way: someone is doing something by hand, at scale, at an unreasonable hour. I take that thing, wire it to an API, give it logging and a dashboard, and hand back the time. Reporting pipelines that used to take twelve hours now finish in under one. Compliance evidence collects itself. Nobody gets paged for a software update.

I like low-friction architecture: managed identity over secrets, infrastructure as code over click-ops, and documentation a future stranger can actually follow. And I have a healthy, well-earned fear of 3 AM alerts.
